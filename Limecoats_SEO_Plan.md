## 1. SEO REALITY CHECK — WHAT TO EXPECT

Before tactics, the truth:

- **SEO takes 3–6 months to start showing.** Anyone promising rankings in 30 days is selling you something. Real, durable rankings come from a combination of site quality, content depth, local signals, and backlinks — none of which are quick.
- **Limocoat has a 15-year domain age advantage.** They've had limocoat.com since around 2010. Domain age and accumulated backlinks matter to Google. We will outrank them — but on niche terms first (Tadelakt Mumbai, Pearl Silk lime finish), then medium terms (Venetian plaster Mumbai), then the head term (lime plaster Mumbai) last.
- **Mumbai search volume for lime plaster is moderate, not huge.** This is good news — the bar to dominate it is achievable. The bad news is that volume isn't infinite, so SEO must be paired with social/Instagram (already running) and architect outreach to drive enough leads.
- **Hindi/Marathi search volume in this category is small.** Architects and premium homeowners search in English. We'll cover Hindi/Marathi as a defensive layer — anyone who searches in those languages will find us — but English is where 80%+ of the actual leads will come from.

What this means in practice: **Mumbai-first English SEO is your main work. Hindi/Marathi are coverage. Pan-India is opportunistic.**

## 2. THE 5 PILLARS OF MUMBAI SEO FOR LIMECOATS

### Pillar 1 — Local SEO (Mumbai-specific signals)
Google Business Profile claimed, optimised, and active. NAP consistency (Name-Address-Phone identical across all citations). LocalBusiness schema on every page. Mumbai-area landing page (Page 19 in the website plan). Reviews from real Mumbai customers — 20+ Google reviews in the first 6 months.

### Pillar 2 — Service / Finish-page SEO
Each of the 9 finish pages targets one primary keyword + 4–5 long-tails. This is where the highest-intent traffic comes from — someone searching "Venetian plaster Mumbai" is 10× more likely to convert than someone searching "wall finish ideas."

### Pillar 3 — Pillar Content SEO (Why Lime, Lime vs Paint)
Two long-form pillar pages (1,500–2,000 words) that capture mid-funnel research traffic — the homeowner reading at 11pm. These pages also build topical authority and accumulate backlinks.

### Pillar 4 — Blog / Content Marketing
A consistent blog rhythm (one good post every 2 weeks) targeting question-based keywords — "Will lime plaster work in Mumbai humidity?", "How much does Venetian plaster cost in India?", "Is Tadelakt waterproof?" — that capture long-tail traffic and feed the pillar pages.

### Pillar 5 — Off-Page (Backlinks, PR, Partnerships)
The hardest part. Architectural digest features, Houzz India profile, ArchDaily India coverage, design-blog mentions, partnership links from architects' studio sites. Without backlinks, the site won't rank for competitive head terms — full stop.

### 5.2 — Google Reviews Strategy

**Goal: 25+ Google reviews in the first 6 months.**

Why this matters: GBP rankings are a function of (proximity × relevance × prominence), and review count + recency is the single biggest "prominence" factor for local searches. Limocoat has very few public reviews — this is a fight you can win on this metric specifically.

**How to get them:**
- After every project completion, send a personalised WhatsApp message to the client with the Google review link. Sample:
  > *"[Client first name], it was a pleasure finishing your [Bandra apartment / Worli penthouse]. If you have 90 seconds and you're happy with the work, a Google review means the world to us — here's the link: [GBP review URL]. Thank you. — Dipen, Limecoats."*
- Make it dead easy — direct review link, no friction.
- Don't ask twice. One ask. Move on.
- **Never offer discounts for reviews** — Google penalises this and removes reviews.
- Respond to every review within 48 hours, including critical ones (calmly, professionally — Google rewards engagement).

### 6.3 — Blog post format (apply to every post)

- **Length: 1,200–2,000 words.** Google ranks long-form for non-trivial topics.
- **Primary keyword in:** H1, first paragraph, URL slug, meta title, meta description, image alt text, at least one H2.
- **At least one secondary keyword in:** an H2.
- **Internal links:** 3–5 per post, linking to relevant finish pages, the Why Lime pillar, the Lime vs Paint pillar, and the Contact page.
- **External links:** 1–2 to authoritative sources (e.g., a peer-reviewed paper on lime carbonation, a heritage architecture article).
- **Images:** 4–8 per post, all with descriptive alt text including keywords where natural.
- **CTA:** every post ends with a "Visit the Experience Centre / Talk to us" CTA.
- **FAQ section at the end:** 4–6 questions per post, marked up with FAQ schema.
- **Author byline:** Dipen Makwana (or guest authors for specific topics — bring in an architect for one post). Author profiles build E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness — Google's core ranking framework).

### 6.4 — Content distribution

A blog post is wasted if no one reads it. Every post must be distributed:

- **Instagram:** carousel summary of the post + link in bio (or story link). Tag the post's geographic area (e.g., "Bandra").
- **Facebook:** full post link + summary.
- **LinkedIn:** post the article (especially architect-facing posts) on Dipen's personal LinkedIn.
- **WhatsApp Status:** quick image + headline + link.
- **Email newsletter:** when you build the list (capture via the catalog download), every blog post goes out as an email.
- **Repurpose:** every post becomes a 30-second Instagram reel script, a Facebook post, and 2–3 tweets / Threads posts.

## 8. OFF-PAGE SEO — LINK BUILDING & PR

This is where SEO becomes hard, and where most small businesses give up. Don't.

Backlinks (other sites linking to limecoats.com) are still the single biggest ranking factor for competitive head terms like "lime plaster Mumbai." Without them, all the on-page work plateaus around page 2 of Google.

**Goal: 25–40 high-quality backlinks in year one.**

### 8.1 — Tier-A links (high authority, hard to get, worth a lot)

| Target | Approach |
|---|---|
| **Architectural Digest India** | Pitch a project story. AD India loves authentic Indian craft + sustainability angle. Your "India's walls are changing" narrative is editorial gold. |
| **ELLE Decor India** | Same — pitch a homeowner project + the founder story |
| **Dwell** | International, but covers India; pitch the cultural angle |
| **The Architect's Newspaper / ArchDaily India** | Architect-facing publications; pitch a project case study |
| **The Hindu (Lifestyle)** / Mint Lounge / Indian Express Eye | Long-form lifestyle pieces on sustainable luxury |
| **Houzz India** | Set up a full Houzz pro profile + upload all 10 finishes as projects (Houzz links carry weight + drive direct architect traffic) |
| **Better Interiors / Home Review India** | Pitch a project per quarter |
| **Beautiful Homes India** (Asian Paints publication) | Yes, pitch them — they cover finishes broadly |

### 8.2 — Tier-B links (mid authority, achievable)

- **Architect & interior designer studio websites** — when you complete a project for them, ask if you can be linked from their "Suppliers" or "Specifications" page. This is your single best link source. Aim for 10+ in year one.
- **Sustainable building / green architecture blogs** — guest posts on Green Building Council India (IGBC) blog, eco-architecture publications.
- **Local Mumbai design / architecture blogs** — there are several; pitch monthly.
- **Industry directories** — Indian architects' associations, Council of Architecture (COA), Indian Institute of Architects (IIA) — apply for any vendor listing.

### 8.3 — Tier-C links (easy, useful, cumulative)

- All the local citations from Section 5.3 (each is a backlink)
- LinkedIn company page (for the "sameAs" schema signal)
- Instagram bio link
- Facebook page link
- Free Justdial / Sulekha listings
- Free Houzz / Behance / Architizer profiles
- Reply to lime-plaster threads on Quora India and Reddit r/IndiaInvestments / r/HomeImprovementIndia (helpful, not spammy)

### 8.4 — PR strategy

Two PR angles you can pitch all year:

1. **The Cultural Angle:** *"India is returning to the wall finishes its grandmothers used. Why?"* — works for lifestyle press, Sunday supplements, podcasts.
2. **The Sustainability Angle:** *"This Mumbai studio is making walls that absorb CO₂ — and selling them as luxury."* — works for green/sustainability press, ESG publications, business press.

**Action:** Write a 1-page founder/brand press release. Send it (cold email is fine) to 30 publications in month 2. Expect a 5–10% response rate; that's 2–3 features. That's plenty.

### 8.5 — What NOT to do

- Don't buy links. Google catches it; you get penalised.
- Don't do mass directory submissions to PBN-style sites. Same problem.
- Don't do reciprocal "we'll link to you, you link to us" deals — Google devalues them.
- Don't comment-spam blogs.
- Don't pay for "guaranteed first page in 30 days" SEO services. They're either lying or cheating, and you'll pay for it later.

## 11. SEO MISTAKES TO AVOID

To close — the things that will derail you if you let them:

- **Don't keyword-stuff.** Don't write "lime plaster Mumbai" 50 times in one paragraph. Google's algorithm is smarter than 2014. Write naturally; the keywords will land naturally.
- **Don't over-optimise URLs.** `/finishes/venetian` is better than `/best-venetian-plaster-mumbai-india`.
- **Don't duplicate content across pages.** Each page must have a unique angle and unique copy. Two pages targeting the same keyword cannibalise each other.
- **Don't write thin content.** A 200-word "Venetian plaster" page won't rank against Limocoat's longer one. Write real, useful, long content.
- **Don't ignore mobile.** Test every page on a phone before publishing. The Mumbai homeowner is on her phone.
- **Don't forget internal links.** A page with no internal links is an SEO orphan. Every page must link out to 3+ other Limecoats pages.
- **Don't chase volume; chase intent.** "Wall painting Mumbai" gets more searches than "Tadelakt Mumbai" but the second converts 50× better. Quality of traffic > quantity.
- **Don't hide your phone number.** Click-to-call from mobile search results is a major lead source. Visible, clickable, top-of-page.
- **Don't switch SEO agencies every 6 months.** SEO compounds. Pick a strategy, execute it for 12 months, then evaluate.
- **Don't underestimate Google Business Profile.** It is, for a Mumbai-local service business, *the single most important SEO asset*. Treat it accordingly.
