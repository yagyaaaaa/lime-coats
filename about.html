About LimeCoats

Our Story & Mission

Who We Are

LimeCoats is a collective of skilled artisans, architects, and eco-conscious designers dedicated to redefining interior surfaces. We believe walls should do more than stand — they should inspire. Our team brings decades of combined expertise in traditional lime plastering, blending old-world craftsmanship with modern aesthetics.

Founded with a vision to bring the timeless beauty of lime finishes to contemporary spaces, we have grown into a trusted name for luxury wall treatments across India and beyond.

Our Mission

We are on a mission to make sustainable luxury accessible. Every surface we touch becomes a canvas of organic beauty — free from toxins, kind to the planet, and built to last for generations. We believe that choosing eco-friendly materials should never mean compromising on aesthetics.

From luxury residences and boutique hotels to commercial showrooms, we have transformed spaces across the country with textures that tell a story. Each project is a collaboration between our artisans and our clients, resulting in finishes that are truly one-of-a-kind.

Our Craft

Lime plastering is one of the oldest building techniques in the world, used in structures that have stood for millennia. We honour this tradition while pushing its boundaries — experimenting with textures, pigments, and application methods to create finishes that are both timeless and contemporary.

Every finish in our portfolio is hand-applied by trained artisans using European-grade lime and natural mineral pigments. The result is a surface that is alive — it breathes, absorbs carbon dioxide, and develops a beautiful patina over time.
