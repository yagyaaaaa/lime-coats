Why Lime?

The Ecological Difference

Lime plaster has been used for thousands of years, from ancient Roman aqueducts to Renaissance frescoes. Unlike synthetic paints and coatings, lime is a living material — it absorbs carbon dioxide as it cures, actively reducing your carbon footprint. It is naturally antibacterial, breathable, and free of volatile organic compounds (VOCs), making it the healthiest choice for your home and the planet.
