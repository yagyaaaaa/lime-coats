Why Lime?

Ancient material, future thinking. Lime is one of the most sustainable building materials on earth — and one of the most beautiful.

🌿

Zero VOC

No volatile organic compounds — pure, safe air from day one.

✨

Toxin-Free

No synthetic chemicals, plasticisers, or harmful additives. Pure mineral chemistry.

💨

Breathable

Micro-porous surfaces regulate moisture naturally, preventing damp and mould.

🌍

Carbon-Absorbing

Lime re-absorbs CO₂ as it cures — your walls actively sequester carbon.

⛰️

Mineral-Based

Sourced directly from limestone — one of earth's most abundant natural materials.

♻️

Sustainable

Long-lasting, repairable, and fully biodegradable. Built to outlast generations.

🧫

Antibacterial

Natural high alkalinity inhibits bacteria and mould growth — no additives required.

🌱

Organic

100% natural origin, free from petrochemicals and synthetic binders.
